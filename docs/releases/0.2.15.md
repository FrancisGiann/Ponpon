# Ponpon 0.2.15

Ponpon 0.2.15 adds quick income and expense drafts, a compact balance widget, a wallet overview, and a 4 × 2 balance and category widget. Balance widgets share a persistent Show/Hide eye control. The compact widget places it beside the balance label; the 4 × 2 widget keeps the balance prominent with four saved category shortcuts. Settings has a dedicated Widgets page, and History pages filtered transactions while retaining totals and date groups.

Download **Ponpon-0.2.15-arm64.apk** for ARM64 Android 8.0+ (version code **2017**). Package `dev.giann.pocket_entry` and signing identity are preserved. The app tracks manual records and never transfers funds.
